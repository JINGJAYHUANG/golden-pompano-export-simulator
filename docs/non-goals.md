# Non-goals

Golden Pompano Export Simulator does not:

- fetch live fish, freight, FX, tariff or tax data;
- classify goods for customs;
- determine legal Incoterms obligations;
- certify an exporter, factory, importer, carrier or buyer;
- validate food-safety, veterinary, health-certificate or labelling compliance;
- predict mortality, harvest yield, quality rejection or market demand;
- assess buyer creditworthiness or guarantee collection;
- book freight, file customs entries, send quotations or accept contracts;
- provide accounting, tax, legal, insurance, investment or veterinary advice;
- disclose or infer any private farm, supplier, buyer, price, margin or negotiation record.

Its purpose is narrower: make assumptions explicit, keep unit bases consistent, show both sides of the transaction and produce an auditable arithmetic bridge for human review.
