# Precise quotation language guide

Avoid:

```text
USD 5,000 per tonne, CIF.
```

Prefer a complete statement such as:

```text
USD 5.55 per kilogram of glazed product, where glaze is defined as ice divided by glazed-product mass and is modelled at 10%; carton declared product weight is 20 kg excluding carton tare; quantity, named place, Incoterms edition, quote validity, payment terms and all destination charges remain subject to the written offer and contract.
```

The exact commercial wording must be reviewed for the real transaction. The purpose of this guide is to expose missing denominators and assumptions, not to create a legally sufficient quotation.
