# Arithmetic versus commercial safety margin

The quote solver computes an arithmetic break-even or target-margin price under declared assumptions. A commercial safety margin is a separate governance decision.

Potential buffers may need to cover:

- input-price expiry;
- size-grade or processing-yield variance;
- freight surcharges and destination charges;
- inspection, delay, demurrage or detention;
- currency movement;
- quality claims, rejection or discount;
- delayed payment or default;
- tax recovery uncertainty;
- model error and omitted costs.

The package does not recommend a universal markup. The reviewer should identify each material uncertainty, avoid double counting and document why any buffer is included.
