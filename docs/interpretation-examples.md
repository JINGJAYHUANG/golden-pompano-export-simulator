# Interpretation examples

## Correct

> Under the synthetic scenario, seller profit after financing and profit share is positive. The result is conditional on the stated product mass, cost responsibility, tax recovery and payment timing.

## Incorrect

> Golden pompano exports are profitable.

## Correct

> The candidate scenario reduces modelled working-capital need relative to the baseline because the payment balance arrives earlier and selected costs are lower.

## Incorrect

> The candidate contract is safer.

## Correct

> Buyer landed cash cost exceeds economic landed cost because the scenario assumes a recoverable import tax.

## Incorrect

> The buyer will recover the tax.

The report should describe what the model calculated and keep unverified commercial, legal and operational conclusions outside the claim.
