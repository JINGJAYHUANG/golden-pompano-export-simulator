# Quote review checklist

Use this checklist after the model runs and before a human issues a commercial quotation.

## Product and quantity

- [ ] Species, product form, size grades and tolerances are written in the sales specification.
- [ ] The glaze definition and test method are explicit.
- [ ] Carton declared product weight, carton tare and pallet tare are confirmed.
- [ ] The quote states whether quantity is net fish, glazed product, packaged gross, carton, container or shipment.
- [ ] Integer carton and shipment-overfill treatment is agreed.
- [ ] Container payload, volume, airflow and route constraints are checked outside the model.

## Price and currency

- [ ] Unit price and denominator are printed together.
- [ ] Currency and exchange-rate reference date are recorded.
- [ ] Quote validity and adjustment triggers are stated.
- [ ] Payment schedule, deposit, security instrument and late-payment treatment are confirmed.
- [ ] Profit-sharing, referral or agency terms are documented separately.

## Logistics and contract

- [ ] Incoterms label, named place and edition are verified.
- [ ] Every origin and destination cost line has an agreed payer.
- [ ] Freight includes known surcharges and a validity period.
- [ ] Insurance scope, insured value and claims procedure are reviewed.
- [ ] Demurrage, detention, inspection and cold-chain incident allocation are addressed.

## Customs, tax and compliance

- [ ] Exporter, importer and customs-representation roles are confirmed.
- [ ] Tariff classification and customs value are supported by current evidence.
- [ ] Duty and import-tax assumptions are current for the route and date.
- [ ] Recoverable tax is confirmed by a qualified adviser; cash timing is separately planned.
- [ ] Food-safety, veterinary, health-certificate, labelling and traceability requirements are checked.
- [ ] Restricted-party, sanctions and payment-compliance checks are completed where applicable.

## Decision quality

- [ ] Seller profit remains acceptable under the declared downside cases.
- [ ] Peak working-capital need is fundable.
- [ ] Buyer landed cash and economic cost are both communicated when recoverable tax is material.
- [ ] The most decision-sensitive inputs have owners and expiry dates.
- [ ] The run bundle verifies successfully.
- [ ] A named human reviewer approves the final quote; the software never sends or accepts it automatically.
