# Decision reversal tests

A robust review should identify the smallest plausible assumption changes that reverse a decision.

Examples:

- unit price at which seller profit reaches zero;
- raw-fish cost that reduces margin below target;
- freight increase that makes the quote commercially unattractive;
- payment delay that creates an unfinanceable peak deficit;
- tax-recovery change that materially raises buyer economic cost;
- glaze or packaging change that alters the quoted denominator;
- destination charge reassignment that changes which party bears the cost.

The simulator's quote solver and sensitivity tables expose these mechanics. A reviewer should not confuse the computed reversal point with a forecast of what will occur.
