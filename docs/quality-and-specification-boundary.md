# Product quality and specification boundary

The simulator treats product form, size mix, glaze and carton declaration as inputs. It does not test or certify them.

A real quality specification may need to address:

- species and product form;
- individual fish-weight bands and tolerance;
- freezing method and temperature;
- glaze target, tolerance and test method;
- defects, damage, dehydration and foreign matter;
- carton and inner-liner requirements;
- net-weight verification;
- lot identification and traceability;
- inspection, rejection, claims and replacement;
- applicable health, veterinary and labelling documentation.

A correct profit model cannot compensate for an ambiguous or unmet product specification.
