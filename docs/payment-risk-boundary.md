# Payment-risk boundary

The cash-timeline engine models when declared receipts and payments occur. It does not estimate whether a buyer will actually pay.

A real transaction may require separate review of:

- deposit enforceability and refund conditions;
- letter-of-credit wording and document discrepancy risk;
- open-account credit limits;
- bank, country and transfer risk;
- currency convertibility and settlement delay;
- collection fees and financing covenants;
- dispute, rejection and set-off rights;
- insurance or guarantee coverage.

A balance receipt entered on day 30 is a scenario assumption, not evidence of collection. Downside cases should extend or remove the receipt when payment security is uncertain.
