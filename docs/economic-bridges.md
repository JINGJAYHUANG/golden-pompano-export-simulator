# Economic bridges

## Seller bridge

```text
invoice revenue
+ seller credits
+ recoverable seller tax
- seller-paid operating and trade costs
- seller tax cash outflow
- financing cost on negative cash balances
= profit before share

profit before share
- conditional profit share
= profit after share
```

The bridge distinguishes three concepts that are often mixed:

- **profit** — economic surplus under the model;
- **cash balance** — timing of receipts and payments;
- **working-capital need** — maximum cumulative cash deficit before later receipts.

A shipment can be profitable but unfundable. A high deposit can reduce financing cost without changing the invoice. A recoverable tax can preserve economic profit while increasing temporary cash needs.

## Buyer bridge

```text
landed cash cost
= invoice
+ buyer-paid logistics and clearance costs
+ full modelled tax cash outflow

landed economic cost
= landed cash cost
- recoverable buyer tax
- buyer credits
```

The economic view should never replace the cash view. Recoverable tax may be delayed, disputed, capped or unavailable; the model reports both.

## Six unit views

Every bridge is divided by:

1. shipped net-fish kg;
2. glazed-product kg;
3. packaged-gross kg;
4. cartons;
5. containers;
6. shipment count.

Comparisons are valid only when the same numerator, currency, date and denominator are used.
