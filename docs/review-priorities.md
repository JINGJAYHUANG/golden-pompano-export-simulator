# Review priorities

Review findings in this order:

1. incorrect mass or price denominator;
2. missing or wrong payer allocation;
3. customs, tax or recoverability assumption without current evidence;
4. seller cash deficit exceeding available funding;
5. payment-security or collection assumption;
6. freight, terminal or cold-chain scope mismatch;
7. product-quality and specification ambiguity;
8. sensitivity capable of reversing the quote decision;
9. rounding, presentation and documentation issues.

A high-impact denominator or contract error cannot be offset by polished reports or attractive profit.
