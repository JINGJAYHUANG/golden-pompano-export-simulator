# Failure modes

A result can be mathematically correct and still unusable because:

- glaze uses a different denominator than the contract;
- carton declaration differs from measured shipment weight;
- size mix is unavailable at harvest;
- a cost line is charged on the wrong physical basis;
- a local destination fee is omitted or assigned to the wrong party;
- customs value or tariff classification is wrong;
- tax assumed recoverable is not recoverable or is recovered much later;
- payment is delayed, disputed or defaulted;
- freight expires or excludes surcharges;
- payload weight is feasible but reefer volume or airflow is not;
- quality inspection, rejection or claims change sale proceeds;
- exchange rates move before settlement;
- a profit-share formula applies to revenue rather than profit;
- multiple stress assumptions double count the same exposure.

The correct response is not to hide these risks in a higher markup. Identify, evidence, allocate and stress them explicitly.
