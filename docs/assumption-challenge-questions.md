# Assumption challenge questions

Before accepting a result, ask:

- Is glaze defined on product mass or fish mass?
- Does carton declared weight include ice and exclude packaging tare?
- Are quantity tolerances compatible with integer cartons?
- Is the raw-fish cost tied to the same size mix and delivery point?
- Does freight include reefer, fuel, peak-season and local surcharges?
- Does the quoted Incoterms named place match the cost allocation?
- Is customs value consistent with the actual invoice and route?
- Is tax recovery legally available, operationally claimed and timed realistically?
- Does the payment schedule reflect security and default risk?
- Is profit share calculated before or after finance, credits and tax recovery?
- Can the seller fund the maximum cash deficit?
- Are buyer alternatives compared on identical mass, currency and date bases?
- Which plausible change makes the decision fail?

Questions without evidence should remain unresolved assumptions, not be silently replaced by convenient numbers.
